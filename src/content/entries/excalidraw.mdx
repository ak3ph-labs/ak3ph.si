Excalidraw is an open-source virtual whiteboard with a hand-drawn look. This instance runs from a fork in `ak3ph-labs` that keeps the upstream license and credit. The fork is rebranded and the Excalidraw+ promotions are removed; everything else follows upstream.

Drawings stay in your browser. Live collaboration and share links still go through Excalidraw's public servers.

It used to be listed on labs.ak3ph.dev and now lives here.
