## What it is

[Excalidraw](https://excalidraw.com) is an open-source virtual whiteboard with a hand-drawn look, good for quick sketches, architecture diagrams and wireframes. This instance is at draw.ak3ph.si and is open to anyone, with no account.

It used to be listed on [labs.ak3ph.dev](https://labs.ak3ph.dev) and moved here when ak3ph.si became the home for self-hosted tools.

## What changed in the fork

The fork in [`ak3ph-labs/excalidraw`](https://github.com/ak3ph-labs/excalidraw) keeps the upstream MIT license and credit. It changes two things:

- **Branding**: the app is rebranded for draw.ak3ph.si.
- **Excalidraw+ promotions removed**: the upsell links and banners for the paid product are gone.

Everything else follows upstream, and the changes are kept small so upstream updates still merge cleanly.

## How it runs

- Built from the fork's `master` branch with a Railway-specific Dockerfile: a Node build stage, then nginx serving the static app.
- One Railway service capped at 0.5 vCPU and 512 MB, behind Cloudflare.
- No volume and no database. Drawings are saved in your browser's local storage, so they stay on your device.

## Known limits

- **Collaboration and share links use Excalidraw's public servers.** Live collaboration rooms and "shareable link" exports go through the upstream collaboration and storage services, not through ak3ph.si. Content is end-to-end encrypted by Excalidraw, but those servers are run by the Excalidraw team, not here.
- **Clearing site data deletes drawings.** Since nothing is stored server-side, export anything you want to keep (`.excalidraw` or PNG/SVG).
- **Upstream changes arrive when the fork is synced**, not automatically.
